# tapelooptoolbox
Automatically calculate exact tapeloop lengths to find BPM options that fit your tapeloop perfectly!

Step 1: Record a single short and loud 'sound' (like a clap) to a completely blank tape loop.

Step 2: Play back the loop over speakers at a reasonable volume.

Step 3: Visit tapelooptoolbox.com and press "Start Listening."
You may need to permit microphone use by your browser. If the app doesn't detect your 'sound,' adjust speaker volume or app sensitivity as needed (or record a new louder sound).

Step 4: Let your loop play until your single 'sound' has been detected by the webapp twice.
Once that single 'sound' is detected twice, active listening will stop and BPM & bar length options will be automatically calculated and displayed.

You can also use the 'manual mode' tab to convert known measurements (inches/cm/seconds). If you have auto detected a tapeloop length, it will auto populate the 'manual mode' so you can see the conversion from seconds to inches/cm.
